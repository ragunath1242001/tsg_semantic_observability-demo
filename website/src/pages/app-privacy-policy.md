# TSG Mobile Wallet Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2025-06-03_

## 1. Personal Data Collection

Our app does **not** collect or store any personal data, except for data included in verifiable credentials issued by external parties. These credentials contain claims an issuer makes about you that it has verified. This may include information such as your name, date of birth, address, email address, and other relevant details. This data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or any third party without your explicit consent. Explicit consent is provided when you choose to present your credentials to a third party, such as a verifier or service provider. This always happens through a secure, user-initiated action within the app.

## 2. Data Collection Methods

All personal data within the app is obtained through verifiable credentials provided by external issuers. The app does not collect data through user input, automatic collection, or third-party integrations beyond these credentials.

## 3. Use of Collected Data

The data contained in verifiable credentials is used solely for the purpose of allowing you to manage, view, and present your credentials as you choose. No data is used for analytics, marketing, or customer support purposes.

## 4. Data Sharing

Your data does **not** leave the app unless you explicitly choose to present your credentials to a third party. We do not share your data with any third parties.

## 5. Data Storage and Protection

All credential data is stored securely on your device. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encryption and access controls.

## 6. Data Retention

Credential data remains on your device until you choose to delete it or uninstall the app. We do not retain any copies of your data.

## 7. Cookies and Tracking

Our app does **not** use cookies or similar tracking technologies.

## 8. User Control

You have full control over your credentials. You can view, manage, or delete your credentials at any time within the app.

## 9. Children’s Privacy

Our app is not specifically intended for children. We do not knowingly collect or store data from children under the age of 16.

## 10. Data Deletion

You can delete your credentials at any time through the app. Deleting the app will also remove all locally stored data, including your credentials.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We will notify users of any changes to this privacy policy via in-app notifications.

## 12. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please contact us at maarten.kollenstart@tno.nl or via the contact page.
